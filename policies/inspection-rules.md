# Sample inspection instructions

**Synthetic instructions for the fictional demo merchant.**

Record one condition: sealed, opened, damaged, or unknown. Record the observation and who entered it. This prototype does not certify product safety.

A reported condition is an unverified claim. If the merchant has not verified it, label the route conditional and require the merchant to confirm the relevant facts before approval. A broken seal overrides the original sealed description. Damage blocks direct forwarding. Uncertainty requires review.

For RET-002, first let the system propose a buyer route. Before approving or dispatching it, record: “Seal is broken” with condition opened. The old proposal must become invalid immediately. The buyer reservation must be released, and inspection should be considered.

Do not mark an item dispatched during this exercise. An already dispatched shipment requires an exception workflow and cannot be undone merely by changing a record.

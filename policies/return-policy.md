# Sample return policy

**Synthetic policy for a fictional merchant. It is not a real business policy.**

Version: `demo-policy-v1`. These passage IDs match the Moss seed records.

## POL-001: Return eligibility

Accept returns within 30 days for this fictional merchant. All seeded cases are 10 days after purchase.

## POL-002: Direct forwarding

A sealed returned item may go to a waiting buyer only when the SKU matches, the arrival meets the buyer deadline, and the merchant approves the current plan.

## POL-003: Opened items

An opened item must undergo inspection before resale. A broken seal blocks forwarding to a waiting buyer, even if the original return form said sealed.

## POL-004: Damaged items

A damaged item must not go directly to a waiting buyer. Route it to an approved inspection or warehouse destination, or a merchant-verified repair business.

## POL-005: Uncertain condition

If condition is unknown or contradictory, do not assume it is sealed. Request evidence or inspection before resale.

## POL-006: External destinations

A search result is only a candidate. The merchant must verify acceptance and costs before an external business becomes an actionable destination.

## POL-007: Approval and current facts

Approval applies only to the current case and policy version. New inspection facts or a policy change require a fresh review and approval.

## POL-008: Reservation limits

A returned item and a waiting order may each have only one active reservation. An expired reservation cannot support an approved shipment.
